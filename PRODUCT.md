# Product

## Register

product

## Users
AUS students (choosing courses, planning degrees, checking prerequisites) and curious faculty/alumni. They arrive from a shared link on a phone or open it on a laptop between classes. Jobs: look up a course or instructor, trace a prerequisite chain, browse 20 years of trends for fun.

## Product Purpose
A static site that turns 20 years of AUS course data (75k sections, 2k instructors, 156k prerequisite links) into a story first and a tool second. Two pages share one data file: the story page (`/`, 8 chapters, each with one scrollytelling lead chart and a few supporting charts) and the explore page (`/explore/`, course, instructor and degree explorers, tables, and an index of all charts). Success: a reader leaves with one surprising fact to share, and a student finds a course or instructor in under 10 seconds.

The story page is designed in the brand register (pacing, motion, chapter colour). The explore page follows the product register below.

## Brand Personality
Campus-proud, playful, approachable. Made by an AUS student for AUS students. Confident about the data, warm in tone, never stiff.

## Anti-references
- Dry institutional reports and registrar portals (Banner-style tables, grey forms).
- Generic SaaS admin dashboards (KPI tiles, sidebar nav, blue-grey palette).

## Design Principles
- Data first, delight second: playfulness lives in copy, motion and small touches, never at the cost of chart legibility.
- Everything is a link: courses, instructors and programs cross-link so curiosity has no dead ends.
- Phone is a first-class reader: shared links land on mobile.
- One static page, no backend: speed and offline-friendliness are features.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA. Colour-blind-safe chart palettes, full keyboard access to explorers, honour prefers-reduced-motion and prefers-color-scheme.
